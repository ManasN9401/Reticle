# Historical Event Specification: The Apollo 11 Moon Landing (July 20, 1969)

## Overview
This file outlines the historical event chosen for the poem, extensive research data, precise statistics and figures, and reference sources.

## Chosen Event
The Apollo 11 Lunar Landing on July 20, 1969.

## Key Facts, Figures, and Statistics
1. Saturn V Rocket Height: 363 feet (111 meters).
2. Thrust Generated: 7.5 million pounds (33.4 million Newtons).
3. Travel Distance: 240,000 miles (386,243 kilometers) from Earth to the Moon.
4. Lunar Module (Eagle) Separation & Landing Time: July 20, 1969; touchdown at 20:17 UTC.
5. Remaining Fuel Margin: Approximately 25 to 30 seconds of fuel remaining upon touchdown in the Sea of Tranquility.
6. Computing Power: The Apollo Guidance Computer (AGC) had 72 kilobytes of ROM and 4 kilobytes of RAM (operating at roughly 1.02 MHz, which is millions of times less powerful than a modern smartphone).
7. Global Audience: An estimated 650 million people watched the broadcast worldwide.
8. Lunar Surface EVA (Extravehicular Activity) Duration: Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin spent 2 hours and 31 minutes outside the spacecraft on the lunar surface.
