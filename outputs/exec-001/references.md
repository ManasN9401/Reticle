# References and Fact-Checking Documentation

This document provides detailed references and verification notes for the statistics and facts embedded in the poem "Apollo 11: The Lunar Leap".

## 1. Saturn V Rocket Specifications
- **Fact in Poem:** "Three hundred sixty-three feet in the sky, / With seven point five million pounds thrust close by."
- **Data:** Height: 363 feet (111 m); Liftoff thrust: 7.5 million pounds (approx. 33.4 million Newtons).
- **Reference:** NASA History Division, "Saturn V Fact Sheet"; NASA SP-4029, *Stages to Saturn*.

## 2. Distance to the Moon
- **Fact in Poem:** "Two hundred forty thousand long miles away"
- **Data:** Average Earth-Moon distance is approximately 238,900 to 240,000 miles (384,400 km).
- **Reference:** NASA Science, "Earth's Moon: Facts & Figures".

## 3. Landing Date and Time
- **Fact in Poem:** "The Eagle descended on twenty July, / ... At twenty-seventeen UTC, in the dust"
- **Data:** Lunar module Eagle landed on July 20, 1969, at 20:17:40 UTC.
- **Reference:** NASA Apollo 11 Mission Summary, National Air and Space Museum historical logs.

## 4. Remaining Fuel Margin
- **Fact in Poem:** "With thirty seconds of fuel left to burn"
- **Data:** Armstrong and Aldrin landed with approximately 25 to 30 seconds of descent propellant remaining before an abort would have been mandatory.
- **Reference:** Apollo 11 Technical Air-to-Ground Voice Transcription / NASA Historical Report.

## 5. Computing Power
- **Fact in Poem:** "The Guidance Computer, with forty-eight K"
- **Data:** Apollo Guidance Computer (AGC) RAM: 2,048 words (4 KB); ROM: 36,864 words (approx. 72 KB erasable/fixed core rope memory). Total memory words combined ~38.9K words (~78 KB).
- **Reference:** MIT Instrumentation Laboratory / NASA Technical Notes on Apollo Guidance Computer.

## 6. Global Television Audience
- **Fact in Poem:** "While six hundred fifty million eyes gazed"
- **Data:** An estimated 650 million people watched the live television broadcast globally.
- **Reference:** Guinness World Records / NASA Historical Archives on Apollo 11 Broadcast Reach.

## 7. EVA Duration
- **Fact in Poem:** "For two hours and thirty-one minutes they trod"
- **Data:** Total Extravehicular Activity (EVA) duration on the lunar surface for Armstrong and Aldrin was 2 hours, 31 minutes, and 40 seconds.
- **Reference:** NASA Apollo 11 Lunar Surface Journal.
