# Apollo 11: The Lunar Leap

A seventy-meter column of steel and fire,
The Saturn V lifted humanity's desire,
Three hundred sixty-three feet in the sky,
With seven point five million pounds thrust close by.

Two hundred forty thousand long miles away,
Through black velvet void and solar array,
The Eagle descended on twenty July,
As history watched with a collective sigh.

At twenty-seventeen UTC, in the dust,
They settled in peace with mechanical trust,
With thirty seconds of fuel left to burn,
And millions awaiting the historic return.

The Guidance Computer, with forty-eight K,
Directed the vessel through perilous gray,
While six hundred fifty million eyes gazed,
In wonder and awe, utterly amazed.

For two hours and thirty-one minutes they trod,
Upon the gray regolith, silent and odd,
Where footprints remain in the airless abyss,
A monument forged in a technological kiss.
